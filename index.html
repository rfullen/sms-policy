Apartment 101

Personal assistant messaging, operated by Rocky Fullen

What this is

Apartment 101 is a personal assistant system run by Rocky Fullen on his own hardware. It answers questions, keeps reminders, and reports the status of his home. It is reached by voice call, through its app, and — for anyone who signs up below — by text message.

What texts you would get

Reminders you have asked it to keep, answers to questions you text in, and notices about your own home system. Message frequency varies and is low volume. Nothing is sent for marketing or promotion, and no third party sends messages through this number.
