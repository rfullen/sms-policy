SMS Terms and Conditions

Personal assistant messaging service

What this service does

Apartment 101 is a personal assistant system operated by Rocky Fullen. By text message it sends reminders you have asked it to keep, answers to questions you send it by text, and notices about your own home system.

Who receives messages

Only people who have signed up on the Apartment 101 sign-up page, at the number they entered there. Messages go to no one else, and no third party sends messages through this number.

How you opt in

Consent is given on the Apartment 101 sign-up page. You enter your mobile number and tick a consent checkbox that is unchecked by default, agreeing to receive text messages from Apartment 101 at that number.

That checkbox is a separate control. It is not bundled with acceptance of these Terms or of the Privacy Policy, and consent to receive text messages is not required to use the service — Apartment 101 works over voice call and through its app whether or not you opt in. Consent is never a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is low volume.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Neither this service nor its operator charges for messages; any charges come from your mobile carrier.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.

No marketing

This service sends no marketing or promotional messages, and no messages relating to loans, credit repair, gambling, cannabis, firearms, or any other restricted category.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
