SMS Privacy Policy

Personal assistant messaging service

Who this covers

This service sends text messages to one person: the owner of the account that operates it. There is no public sign-up, no customer list, and no third-party recipient.

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. Information is used solely to deliver messages to the account owner.

What is collected

Only the account owner's own mobile number, entered by the account owner, so that messages can be delivered to it. No numbers are collected from anyone else, and none are obtained from any third party.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is low volume. Messages are sent only in response to a request from the account owner, or as a reminder the account owner has configured.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help.

This policy may be updated. The current version is always the one published at this address.
